BuzzBuddy Program

Saving
bee life

An initiative by Smart Hives for bees that are losing their homes. When land is cleared and the flowers disappear, bees lose their food and their nests. Contact us, and we will take care of the bees.

Contact the BuzzBuddiesWhy it matters

The situation

Bee life matters

Every colony is a community of thousands of bees working as one. When they thrive, so do the crops, gardens and wild plants around them. When a colony is lost, much more is lost than honey.

For anyone who has seen their bees disappear, or watched the land around them change, that loss is real. It is why the BuzzBuddy Program exists.

The problem

Loss of
habitat

Clearing land and reducing flowering plants means bees lose food and nesting areas.

Without flowers to feed on and safe places to nest, colonies weaken, move on, or are lost. Instead of leaving bees to cope alone, you can bring them to us.

How it works
